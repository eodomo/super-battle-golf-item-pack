# Item Pack

Custom items for Super Battle Golf that behave like the game's own: they come from item boxes, sit in the hotbar, and are used, thrown or ridden against other players.

## Language

**Pickup**:
One item received from an item box, or picked up after being dropped. Each pickup has a fixed number of uses.
_Avoid_: Copy, charge

**Use**:
One spend of a pickup's allowance. A use that misses still counts, except for the exceptions below.
_Avoid_: Shot, charge

**Reusable weapon**:
An item whose swings do not spend a use when they miss: the Lightsaber's swings. The Jellyfish Net is not one.

**Free miss**:
A Lightsaber or Captain America's Shield throw that hits terrain instead of a player or cart: it returns to the thrower's hand and the use is kept. A throw that hits nothing at all is lost.

**Knockout**:
A player is ragdolled and recovers on their own. This is what most hits do.
_Avoid_: Stun

**Elimination**:
A player is sent back to their spawn. The Lightsaber and the Kamehameha do this; the game calls it the same.
_Avoid_: Kill

**Knockout immunity**:
The game's short window after a knockout during which the same player can't be knocked out again.

**Shield**:
Protection against hits. The Bubble Shield and Captain America's Shield are meant to be as effective as the game's own electromagnet shield; Captain America's only blocks hits from in front of the wearer.
