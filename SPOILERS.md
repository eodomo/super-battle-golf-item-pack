# Spoilers: what's in the pack

The README keeps the items a surprise. Everything is here instead.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Banana Peel** | Use to drop a peel at your feet; hold aim + use to throw one. Anyone who runs over a peel slips: a leg-sweep knockout that launches them forward (shields and knockout immunity still protect you). Golf carts and the Megazord that drive over one spin out Mario Kart style: a hop, two spins and most of their speed gone. You can't slip on your own peel for the first 2 seconds. Any damage that reaches a landed peel (shots, explosions, beams, saber) clears it. A thrown peel flies through portals. One peel per pickup. |
| **Portal Gun** | Left click places a blue portal where you're looking, right click an orange one, like Portal (with Portal's two-color crosshair). Each player has their own pair. You can see through linked portals, and players, balls, carts and items that go in come out the other portal with their momentum. Like in Portal you can stand halfway in one, with your other half poking out of the other portal; you only go over once your middle passes through. Dueling pistol and elephant gun shots, rockets, freeze bombs and boogie bombs go through linked portals too, so you can shoot someone on the other side. Portals last until replaced or until the hole ends. Shoot as much as you like: the gun is used up once you've placed both a blue and an orange portal. Each shot has a wooshy sound everyone can hear. |
| **Steam Whistle** | Blow it and a winged blue tank engine swoops in and sets an opponent on fire: the one closest to your crosshair, at any range (never a teammate). While you hold the whistle, the game's lock-on marker shows who it will be. If nobody's left to target, it picks a random other player (or you, if you're alone). A tribute to a famous Skyrim mod. |
| **Sniper Rifle** | Not aiming, it is carried at low ready, the stock tucked under your right arm. Halo's SRS99, recreated. Hold aim to scope in at 2x (scroll up or middle mouse for 10x) with a Halo-style scope view; use to fire. One shot knocks a player out and goes through every player in line (and through portals). One shot per pickup. Everyone hears the shot across the map and sees the vapor trail. |
| **Predator Missile** | MW2's killstreak. Left click opens the laptop and launches: your view becomes the missile, coming down from high above. Steer with the mouse, left click or space boosts once. It explodes on whatever it hits with a big rocket blast; everyone else sees and hears it coming. Your golfer stands still while you fly. |
| **Kamehameha** | Hold left click to charge (up to 3 s), release to fire a beam where you're looking. Hold right click to aim like the dueling pistol (its crosshair, slower walk, closer camera), also while charging. Everyone in it is eliminated (the beam is wider and golf balls are blasted harder the longer you charged). At full power the coffee's timer wheel counts down the 4 seconds left before it fires by itself. Walls and terrain stop it, but players do not: it goes through everyone in its path and destroys golf carts (eliminating their riders). While you channel you levitate: walking moves you the way you are looking (up and down too; hold space to climb and left control to descend), slower than walking, enough to cross a gap. One per pickup. |
| **Dirt Blocks** | Minecraft building: your first click starts a 5 second timer (shown on the coffee's timer wheel), and until it runs out you have unlimited blocks (the item is used when it ends; the blocks stay). Right click places a block on the grid where you're looking (against the face of the block you're pointing at). Blocks are solid for players and balls; a white outline shows where the next one goes. Any damage breaks them: golf swings, shots, explosions, beams (or left click them). |
| **Blue Shell** | Mario Kart. Use it and it goes after whoever's ball is closest to the hole (even you, if you're in the lead). It flies high over the course with its siren wailing for everyone to hear, circles over the leader and slams down: a big blue explosion on them, and their ball gets knocked away from the hole. In the Driving Range (no hole) it picks another player, or you if you're alone. |
| **Boogie Bomb** | Fortnite. Throw the disco ball: it flies on the same arc as a freeze bomb shot and goes through portals. It bounces, pops into a disco party, and everyone caught in the blast does their victory dance for 5 seconds, unable to move or use items. Electromagnet shields protect you. One per pickup. |
| **Lightsaber** | Star Wars. You get a blue, green or red one at random; it ignites and hums in your hand. Use to slash: a quick sideways swipe (alternating forehand and backhand, some level, some angled; aim to swing where you're looking). Whoever it hits is killed (sent back to their spawn) and falls apart in two pieces at the cut, with glowing, cauterized edges, until they respawn. Golf carts get cut in half and destroyed, and everyone in them is knocked out. Swing as much as you like; the first hit uses it up. Hold right click to aim (the pistol's crosshair; you take a ready stance, left arm out front, the blade lowered behind you just above the ground) and left click to throw it Battlefront style: it spins out where you aim, cuts the first person (or cart) it touches, and if it hits nobody it comes back to your hand like a boomerang (sooner if it hits a wall). The hum is steady and quiet while you walk, with a soft whoosh when you turn and the full swing sound when you swing. |
| **Web Slinger** | Spider-Man. A red-and-blue web shooter glove. Your first click starts a 5 second timer (shown on the coffee's timer wheel); until it runs out, hold left click to shoot a web where you aim (a little aim assist helps with thin things). It sticks and you swing from it like a pendulum, reeling in a bit as you go; let go of the button to release and keep your speed. Everyone sees the strand and hears "thwip". Webbing another player, a cart or a loose item pulls it toward you instead of swinging you. Webs go through portals. The item is used up when the timer ends. |
| **Megazord** | Power Rangers. A ride you climb into: a giant Mighty Morphin Megazord (red Tyrannosaurus chest with silver teeth, black and yellow wing shoulders, the Power Sword in one hand, the Mastodon shield on the other arm, a yellow Sabertooth Tiger leg and a blue Triceratops leg) stands up where you are and you get inside its cockpit (you're hidden while you ride; another player can take the second seat with the interact key). It is slow and heavy (stomps shake the camera and kick up dust). Left click swings the sword in a slow, wide low slash that sends carts flying (the driver only; no horn): anyone on foot in front of it is knocked out, and the driver's own ball is smacked away (other players' balls are left alone). Right click strikes a battle pose with a war cry. Under the armor it is a real golf cart: it drives, bounces and runs people over like one; it's as big to hit as it looks, and if it rolls over it stays down. Models: `blender/Zord/`, code: `src/Items/Zord/`. |
| **Bubble Shield** | Halo 3. Plant it at your feet and a glowing amber honeycomb dome pops up around it for 10 seconds. Nothing crosses its edge: shots, rockets, explosions, beams, swings, freezes and boogie bombs from outside can't knock out anyone inside, and nobody inside can knock out anyone outside; golf balls and carts (the Megazord too) bounce off it. Anyone on foot can walk in and out, and people inside can still fight each other. It flashes when it stops something and collapses when its time is up. |
| **Captain America's Shield** | Marvel. Worn on your right hand, facing out from your chest, while you hold right click it blocks a hit coming from in front of you (shots, swings, rockets, explosions, beams), with a ringing clang everyone hears. Hold right click to aim, then left click to throw it at the crosshair; it keeps going until it hits something, then comes back to your hand. Hitting a player knocks them out; hitting a cart knocks out whoever is riding in it. One charge: a block, or a throw that hits a player or cart, uses it up (once it's back in your hand). Bouncing off a wall or the ground is free, but a throw that hits nothing at all is gone for good. |
| **Ender Pearl** | Minecraft. Throw it (it flies on the same arc as a freeze bomb shot and goes through portals) and you teleport to where it lands, with a "vwoop" and purple particles at both ends. Land it on a wall and you end up at the foot of it. One per pickup. |
| **Emergency Meeting** | Among Us. Press the button: everyone gets an "EMERGENCY MEETING!" screen and a klaxon, then every player is pulled to a round table in front of you and stands frozen around it for 3 seconds (you can still look around). Their balls stay where they were, so everyone else has a walk back. The screen and klaxon are the pack's own; put `emergency_meeting.png` and/or `emergency_klaxon` (`.ogg`, `.wav`, `.mp3` or `.flac`) in `BepInEx\plugins\ItemPack\EmergencyMeeting\` to use your own. |
| **Jellyfish Net** | SpongeBob. Held out to your right; one big swing across your body catches whoever is close in front of you at any point of the sweep. Whoever it catches is curled up in the net, kicking and shoving against it, and carried out in front of you for 10 seconds while you walk at about half speed; left click flings them out early. Put the net away and they drop out. A swing that catches nobody uses the net up. Swooshes as it swings and as it lets go (replace them with `jellyfishnet_swing` / `jellyfishnet_release` sound files in `BepInEx\plugins\ItemPack\JellyfishNet\`). |

## Outfit pieces

**Power Ranger set** (not items: three pieces in the outfit house menu, free, no credits needed). *Ranger Helmet* (hats tab) has a dark visor, silver chin guard and a gold ridge, and covers hair, face items and ears. *Ranger Suit* (clothes tab) has a white chest chevron, gold belt with a silver buckle and white gloves. *Ranger Boots* (shoes tab) are white with a colored cuff. *Power Sword* (clubs tab) replaces your golf club with a gold-hilted sword whose stripe and grip wraps take the chosen color. Each comes in red, blue, yellow, pink, black and green (pick the color with the swatches like any other clothing; red and blue are the only ones allowed in team matches). Everyone in a match needs the pack to see them. The suit and boots are built when the game starts from the game's own suit and work boot models, so nothing from the game is shipped in the pack. Models: `blender/PowerRanger/` (build_helmet.py, build_icons.py), code: `src/Cosmetics/`.

## How often they show up

Each item turns up where it's useful. The game picks an item box pool by how far behind the leader you are (close: under 50 m, medium: 50-125 m, far: 125-200 m, very far: 200 m+), and the pack makes up roughly 30% of each pool:

- **Near the lead:** fighting items: banana peel, boogie bomb, lightsaber, jellyfish net, dirt blocks, kamehameha, the two shields (bubble and Captain America's), and now and then the sniper rifle, portal gun and web slinger.
- **Further back:** the sniper rifle, portal gun, web slinger, ender pearl, steam whistle, predator missile and Megazord.
- **Far behind:** the blue shell joins in, so does the emergency meeting (it pulls the leaders back to you), the Megazord (a ride) gets more common, and the close-range fighting items stop showing up.
- **Walked past your ball:** now and then a portal gun, web slinger or ender pearl, alongside the game's coffee and spring boots.
- **Driving Range:** everything.

Hosts can change the odds in the match setup's item probability sliders like any other item (the pack's items are listed after the game's), and scale the starting values with `ItemBoxFrequency` in the settings file. The host's `BepInEx\LogOutput.log` lists every item box roll (who, what, from which pool).

## Replaceable sounds

**Steam Whistle theme:** the pack plays its own built-in anthem. To use different music, put an audio file named `thomas_theme.ogg` (or `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.flac`) in `BepInEx\plugins\ItemPack\SteamWhistle\`. Each player hears their own file, and it's never committed to this repo.

**Sniper Rifle sounds:** the pack synthesizes its own shot, zoom and reload sounds. To use others (for example ones from your own copy of Halo), put `sniper_fire`, `sniper_zoom_in`, `sniper_zoom_out` and/or `sniper_reload` (`.ogg`, `.wav`, `.mp3` or `.flac`) in `BepInEx\plugins\ItemPack\SniperRifle\`. Each player uses their own files; they're never committed or shipped.

**Other replaceable sounds:** every new item synthesizes its own sounds, and each can be replaced the same way with a file in the item's folder: `predator_launch`, `predator_loop`, `predator_boost` (in `PredatorMissile\`), `kamehameha_charge` and `kamehameha_fire` (in `Kamehameha\`; a voice line works well for the charge), `dirt_place` and `dirt_break` (in `DirtBlocks\`), `blueshell_launch`, `blueshell_siren` and `blueshell_impact` (in `BlueShell\`), `boogie_pop` and `boogie_music` (in `BoogieBomb\`; e.g. the real boogie bomb music), `lightsaber_hum`, `lightsaber_ignite`, `lightsaber_swing` and `lightsaber_cut` (in `Lightsaber\`), `webslinger_thwip` and `webslinger_snap` (in `WebSlinger\`), `bubble_deploy`, `bubble_hum`, `bubble_block` and `bubble_collapse` (in `BubbleShield\`), `cap_throw`, `cap_clang` and `cap_catch` (in `CapShield\`), `pearl_throw` and `pearl_teleport` (in `EnderPearl\`). Audio files are git-ignored and never packaged.
