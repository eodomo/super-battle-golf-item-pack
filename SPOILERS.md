# Spoilers: what's in the pack

The README keeps the items a surprise. Everything is here instead.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Banana Peel** | Use to drop a peel at your feet; hold aim + use to throw one. Anyone who runs over a peel slips: a leg-sweep knockout that launches them forward (shields and knockout immunity still protect you). You can't slip on your own peel for the first 2 seconds. One peel per pickup. |
| **Portal Gun** | Left click places a blue portal where you're looking, right click an orange one, like Portal (with Portal's two-color crosshair). Each player has their own pair. You can see through linked portals, and players, balls, carts and items that go in come out the other portal with their momentum. Like in Portal you can stand halfway in one, with your other half poking out of the other portal; you only go over once your middle passes through. Dueling pistol and elephant gun shots, rockets, freeze bombs and boogie bombs go through linked portals too, so you can shoot someone on the other side. Portals last until replaced or until the hole ends. Shoot as much as you like: the gun is used up once you've placed both a blue and an orange portal. Each shot has a wooshy sound everyone can hear. |
| **Steam Whistle** | Blow it and a winged blue tank engine swoops in and sets a player on fire (a random other player by default). A tribute to a famous Skyrim mod. |
| **Sniper Rifle** | Halo's SRS99, recreated. Hold aim to scope in at 2x (scroll up or middle mouse for 10x) with a Halo-style scope view; use to fire. One shot knocks a player out and goes through up to 2 players. One shot per pickup. Everyone hears the shot across the map and sees the vapor trail. |
| **Predator Missile** | MW2's killstreak. Left click opens the laptop and launches: your view becomes the missile, coming down from high above. Steer with the mouse, left click or space boosts once. It explodes on whatever it hits with a big rocket blast; everyone else sees and hears it coming. Your golfer stands still while you fly. |
| **Kamehameha** | Hold left click to charge (up to 3 s), release to fire a beam where you're looking. Hold right click to aim like the dueling pistol (its crosshair, slower walk, closer camera), also while charging. Everyone in it gets knocked out and launched (harder the longer you charged); golf balls get blasted too. Walls and terrain stop it. One per pickup. |
| **Dirt Blocks** | Minecraft building: your first click starts a 5 second timer (shown on the coffee's timer wheel), and until it runs out you have unlimited blocks (the item is used when it ends; the blocks stay). Right click places a block on the grid where you're looking (against the face of the block you're pointing at). Blocks are solid for players and balls; a white outline shows where the next one goes. Any damage breaks them: golf swings, shots, explosions, beams (or left click them). |
| **Blue Shell** | Mario Kart. Use it and it goes after whoever's ball is closest to the hole (even you, if you're in the lead). It flies high over the course with its siren wailing for everyone to hear, circles over the leader and slams down: a big blue explosion on them, and their ball gets knocked away from the hole. In the Driving Range (no hole) it picks another player, or you if you're alone. |
| **Boogie Bomb** | Fortnite. Throw the disco ball: it flies on a quick, flat arc and lands where your crosshair points (or as far as it reaches toward it), and goes through portals. It bounces, pops into a disco party, and everyone caught in the blast does their victory dance for 5 seconds, unable to move or use items. Electromagnet shields protect you. One per pickup. |
| **Lightsaber** | Star Wars. You get a blue, green or red one at random; it ignites and hums in your hand. Use to slash: a quick sideways swipe (alternating forehand and backhand, some level, some angled; aim to swing where you're looking). Whoever it hits is knocked out and falls apart in two pieces at the cut, with glowing, cauterized edges, until they get back up. Golf carts get cut in half and destroyed, and everyone in them is knocked out. Swing as much as you like; the first hit uses it up. Right click throws it Battlefront style: it spins out where you aim, cuts the first person (or cart) it touches, and if it hits nobody it comes back to your hand like a boomerang (sooner if it hits a wall). The hum is steady and quiet while you walk, with a soft whoosh when you turn and the full swing sound when you swing. |
| **Web Slinger** | Spider-Man. A red-and-blue web shooter glove. Your first click starts a 5 second timer (shown on the coffee's timer wheel); until it runs out, hold left click to shoot a web where you aim (a little aim assist helps with thin things). It sticks and you swing from it like a pendulum, reeling in a bit as you go; let go of the button to release and keep your speed. Everyone sees the strand and hears "thwip". The item is used up when the timer ends. |
| **Megazord** | Power Rangers. A ride you climb into: a giant Mighty Morphin Megazord (red Tyrannosaurus chest with silver teeth, black and yellow wing shoulders, the Power Sword in one hand, the Mastodon shield on the other arm, a yellow Sabertooth Tiger leg and a blue Triceratops leg) stands up where you are and you get inside its cockpit (you're hidden while you ride; another player can take the second seat with the interact key). Drive it and it stomps along; right click strikes a battle pose with a war cry. Under the armor it is a real golf cart: it drives, bounces and runs people over like one, and it stands itself back up if it rolls over. Models: `blender/Zord/`, code: `src/Items/Zord/`. |

## Outfit pieces

**Power Ranger set** (not items: three pieces in the outfit house menu, free, no credits needed). *Ranger Helmet* (hats tab) has a dark visor, silver chin guard and a gold ridge, and covers hair, face items and ears. *Ranger Suit* (clothes tab) has a white chest chevron, gold belt with a silver buckle and white gloves. *Ranger Boots* (shoes tab) are white with a colored cuff. *Power Sword* (clubs tab) replaces your golf club with a gold-hilted sword whose stripe and grip wraps take the chosen color. Each comes in red, blue, yellow, pink, black and green (pick the color with the swatches like any other clothing; red and blue are the only ones allowed in team matches). Everyone in a match needs the pack to see them. The suit and boots are built when the game starts from the game's own suit and work boot models, so nothing from the game is shipped in the pack. Models: `blender/PowerRanger/` (build_helmet.py, build_icons.py), code: `src/Cosmetics/`.

## How often they show up

The banana peel and boogie bomb are as common as the landmine (near the lead and in the Driving Range) and still turn up now and then further back. Everything else is about as rare as the orbital laser and thunderstorm, and like those it only comes out of item boxes for players who are behind the leader (more often the further behind you are). The Driving Range gives everything now and then. Hosts can change the odds in the match setup's item probability sliders like any other item (the pack's items are listed after the game's), and change the starting values with `ItemBoxFrequency` in the settings file.

## Replaceable sounds

**Steam Whistle theme:** the pack plays its own built-in anthem. To use different music, put an audio file named `thomas_theme.ogg` (or `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.flac`) in `BepInEx\plugins\ItemPack\SteamWhistle\`. Each player hears their own file, and it's never committed to this repo.

**Sniper Rifle sounds:** the pack synthesizes its own shot, zoom and reload sounds. To use others (for example ones from your own copy of Halo), put `sniper_fire`, `sniper_zoom_in`, `sniper_zoom_out` and/or `sniper_reload` (`.ogg`, `.wav`, `.mp3` or `.flac`) in `BepInEx\plugins\ItemPack\SniperRifle\`. Each player uses their own files; they're never committed or shipped.

**Other replaceable sounds:** every new item synthesizes its own sounds, and each can be replaced the same way with a file in the item's folder: `predator_launch`, `predator_loop`, `predator_boost` (in `PredatorMissile\`), `kamehameha_charge` and `kamehameha_fire` (in `Kamehameha\`; a voice line works well for the charge), `dirt_place` and `dirt_break` (in `DirtBlocks\`), `blueshell_launch`, `blueshell_siren` and `blueshell_impact` (in `BlueShell\`), `boogie_pop` and `boogie_music` (in `BoogieBomb\`; e.g. the real boogie bomb music), `lightsaber_hum`, `lightsaber_ignite`, `lightsaber_swing` and `lightsaber_cut` (in `Lightsaber\`), `webslinger_thwip` and `webslinger_snap` (in `WebSlinger\`). Audio files are git-ignored and never packaged.
